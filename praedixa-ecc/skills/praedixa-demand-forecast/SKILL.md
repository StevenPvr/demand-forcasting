## Repo Rules To Apply

- Stay anchored in the current wedge first:
  - demand forecasting
  - staffing forecasting
- Target the current GTM surface first:
  - restaurant franchisees
  - quick service
  - bakeries/snacking
  - coffee shops
  - dark kitchens
  - other perishable food operators
- Frame the value around concrete pain:
  - poor volume anticipation
  - overproduction
  - waste
  - stockouts
  - cost matter
  - staffing mismatch
  - margin pressure
- Do not present Praedixa as BI, ERP, or a generic forecasting widget.
- Do not oversell end-to-end autonomous decisioning when the task is still prediction-centric.
- Keep the long-term decision-layer vision visible only if it clarifies, not if it blurs the wedge.

## Workflow

1. Anchor the task in the current wedge: demand forecasting first.
2. Clarify the operational grain: site, product, family, or service window.
3. Translate technical quality into waste, stockout, cost-matter, and margin implications.
4. Avoid overstating autonomous decisioning if the task is still prediction-centric.
5. End with a business-readable summary, not only model language.

## Output

Prefer a technical summary plus a Praedixa-style business translation memo.
