# Praedixa ROI Translation

## Read First

- `praedixa-ecc/rules/praedixa/business-framing.md`
- `praedixa-ecc/templates/business-translation-memo.md`
- `praedixa-ecc/templates/decision-log.md`

## Repo Rules To Apply

- Start from measured evidence, not from marketing language.
- Translate forecasting quality into plausible operational outcomes:
  - lower waste
  - fewer stockouts
  - better cost matter control
  - better staffing alignment
  - better margin
- Separate:
  - observed impact
  - plausible impact under assumptions
  - long-term vision
- Use ROI language carefully in early-stage contexts.
- Do not present large EBITDA improvement claims as established facts without proof.

## Workflow

1. Start from measured model evidence, not marketing copy.
2. Translate technical movement into operational outcomes with explicit assumptions.
3. Separate observed ROI from plausible ROI.
4. Keep the message anchored in the wedge: demand and staffing.
5. End with a short decision-oriented memo a founder or operator can reuse directly.

## Output

Produce a business translation memo with assumptions clearly marked.
